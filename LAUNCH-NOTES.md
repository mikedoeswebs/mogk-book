# Launch notes

Short bullets explaining what the new booking system does better than the old way, written so they can drop into a launch email, a "what's new" page, or an internal brief.

## For parents

- Book any session straight from the website with a card payment, instead of checking if there's availabilty and sending un-receipted bank transfers
- Manage every child on one account, with all sessions and your full booking history visible at any time.
- Cancel up to 24 hours before a session and the amount goes onto your account as credit, applied automatically to your next booking. No need to chase a refund.
- Late bookings (under 24 hours) still work, they just need an admin approval rather than being a hard cut-off.
- Email reminder the day before each session, plus an optional heads-up about the next one coming up.
- Sign in by clicking a link sent to your email. No password to remember or reset.
- Your historic attendance from the old records is preserved, so captain and player-of-the-week history carries across.
- Designed to work from your phone, or computer, whichever device you use

## For us

- Stripe handles every payment, refund and receipt. No BACS chasing, no manual reconciliation.
- Session capacity is checked at the moment of booking, so we can't oversell a session or double-book a player.
- Late bookings drop into an approvals queue instead of auto-confirming, so we stay in control of who's coming.
- Cancellations within 24 hours convert to account credit rather than card refunds, keeping income in the club for the next session.
- Historic attendance imported from the old spreadsheets stays attached to each player, so coach reports, awards and stats carry forward unbroken.
- Reminders and "book the next one" prompts go out automatically, reducing no-shows and filling spaces without admin time.
- One dashboard covers sessions, bookings, approvals, coach assignments, session reports and awards, replacing several inboxes and sheets.
